About this design reference

This prototype sketches a card-based, selectable science model. Its chemical values and indicator colours are approximate illustrations, not measurements. It has no verified NaCCA level-specific indicator mapping, no scored activity and no approval to serve as a lesson.

Safety first

Screen only. Never taste any substance, mix unknown chemicals or handle acids, alkalis or wood-ash lye without an appropriately supervised laboratory and safety equipment. Do not use this page as an experiment guide.

Observe, don't score

Select a model substance and an indicator display, then notice how the on-screen colour and approximate pH change. What might a real measurement need that a simple visual model cannot show? Nothing on this page records a response.

These visual conventions are illustrative. Real samples and plant-pigment indicator colours vary; a simulation cannot replace a supervised investigation.
